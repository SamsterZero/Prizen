# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Prizen welcoming, safe, and respectful for everyone. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to act in good faith and help sustain a healthy technical community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and changes, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and clearly stated boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption, impersonation, spam, or abusive promotion
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project discussions, and any space where someone officially represents Prizen.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Enforcement will prioritize safety, proportionality, privacy, and a fair review of available context.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using a contact method listed on the [SamsterZero GitHub profile](https://github.com/SamsterZero). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
